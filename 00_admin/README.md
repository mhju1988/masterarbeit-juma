## Admission rules that actually bite

- **At least 78 CP are required for admission to the Masterarbeit.** Found out in August when the
  Prüfungsamt refused the application at 72 CP. This gates registration — the exposé does not.
- **Six months from registration**, with a four-week extension possible on medical grounds.
- The application goes to `pruefungsamt.informatik@w-hs.de`, **from the `@studmail.w-hs.de`
  address**, carrying both examiners' signatures.
- The topic on the application is a **working title** and may be changed later without formal
  consequence (Prof. Zeume, 2026-08).
- **Second examiner: Prof. Dr. Gleim — confirmed.** He agreed and signed the Antrag; he shaped the
  index-structure angle. The July protocol had also listed Kohn and Anderla.
- **Registered. Bearbeitungszeit: November 2026 to 11.05.2027 inclusive.** Confirmed by the
  Prüfungsamt (Frau Springer). Both examiners signed; 78 CP reached.
- **The clock starts on the day the application is submitted**, not on a later issue of the topic:
  *"Der Start der Bearbeitungszeit ist immer der Tag, an dem Sie den Antrag auf Zulassung stellen."*
